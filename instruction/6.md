# Phase 2A — Minimal 3D Evaporation-Driven Salt Growth CA

Repository: `ndmthio18/salt_grow`

Reviewed commit: `ae4c7ae6`

## Objective

Implement the first independent, CPU-based, three-dimensional cellular automaton prototype for evaporation-driven NaCl growth above the surface of a porous substrate.

Use the existing verified Phase 1/1.1 evaporation and transactional mass-allocation modules.

The goal is to demonstrate spatial growth, strict mass conservation, and basic morphology diagnostics.

Do not implement full PNM flow, a vapor diffusion solver, or permeability calculations.

## Task 1 — Physical state representation

Use separate arrays for material identity and liquid accessibility.

The material array must distinguish:

- Empty space (air or liquid).
- Original porous substrate.
- Precipitated NaCl.

The initial geometry must contain a prescribed substrate interface at the bottom of the domain.

Initialize multiple NaCl crystal seeds on or immediately above the substrate.

Track initial seed mass separately from newly precipitated mass.

Most importantly, distinguish:

**Where solid salt may grow** from **how dissolved salt reaches the growing crystal surface**.

A candidate growth site may be an empty air voxel adjacent to an existing wetted salt crystal.

Do not require the target voxel itself to be liquid-filled.

## Task 2 — Simplified continuous-wetting model

Implement a clearly documented prototype assumption:

All salt crystals that are connected through the existing solid salt network to a wetted seed at the substrate may be treated as having access to a continuous surface liquid film.

This is a conceptual supply-connectivity rule, not a physical film-flow calculation.

The wetted salt network must be recomputed or consistently updated as growth proceeds.

Only candidates adjacent to supply-connected, wetted salt are eligible for growth.

Provide a switch that disables the wetting condition for controlled testing.
